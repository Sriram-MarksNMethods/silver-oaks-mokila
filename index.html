Welcome to Silver Oaks Mokila

Built on a legacy of 25 years and five campuses, Silver Oaks is guided by a simple yet enduring philosophy: Character Before Competence. It is a belief that integrity, empathy and purpose matter just as much as achievement, and one that continues to shape every aspect of the learning experience.

Serving families across Hyderabad’s evolving landscape, Silver Oaks Mokila brings rigorous, values-led education closer to the community. From the early years through Grade 5, young learners will grow in thoughtfully designed spaces, guided by educators who nurture curiosity, encourage questioning and foster independence.

Here, character is not simply taught. It is lived, every day.

From the Dean's Desk

“Destiny of a country begins in its classrooms” is the belief that guides Dr. Seetha Murty's approach to education at Silver Oaks International Schools.

With master’s degrees in education and political science, along with academic programmes at Harvard and Stanford Universities and extensive International Baccalaureate (IB) training, she fondly describes herself as the Principal Learner at Silver Oaks.

She has been instrumental in introducing the International Baccalaureate Programme and championing research-based teaching and learning through cognitive approaches. She also trains school heads and teachers at international schools across the Asia-Pacific region.

Recognised by The Times of India among the state's top three leaders in the Lead India Contest, she draws inspiration from Mahatma Gandhi’s philosophy of personal responsibility and social transformation.

This philosophy continues to shape Silver Oaks as an ecosystem for inspired learning.

FAQs

1. What is Silver Oaks Mokila?+

Silver Oaks Mokila is the newest campus of Silver Oaks International School in Hyderabad, bringing 25 years of educational experience and the philosophy of Character Before Competence to the Mokila community.

2. When will Silver Oaks Mokila open?+

Silver Oaks Mokila is scheduled to open in June 2027 for the 2027–28 academic year.

3. Which grades will be offered at Silver Oaks Mokila?+

The campus will initially welcome students from PP1 to Grade 5, catering to children in their formative and primary learning years.

4. Where is Silver Oaks Mokila located?+

Silver Oaks Mokila is located in Mokila, Hyderabad, making quality, values-led education accessible to families in and around the rapidly developing neighbourhood.

5. What curriculum will Silver Oaks Mokila follow?+

Silver Oaks Mokila's academic offering includes the IB Primary Years Programme (PYP), with the school's campus listing also featuring IB MYP and CBSE. The admissions team can help parents understand the programme availability for each grade.

6. What makes the Silver Oaks approach to education different?+

At Silver Oaks, education goes beyond academic achievement. Guided by the philosophy of Character Before Competence, the school focuses on developing integrity, empathy, curiosity, responsibility and independent thinking alongside academic capabilities.

7. How does Silver Oaks encourage learning beyond textbooks?+

Through inquiry-based learning, experiential activities, collaborative projects and real-world connections, children are encouraged to ask questions, explore ideas, make discoveries and apply their learning meaningfully.

8. How does Silver Oaks nurture a child's overall development?+

Silver Oaks follows an inside-out approach to education, nurturing intellectual, emotional, social and physical development. The emphasis is on helping children grow into confident learners, empathetic individuals and responsible members of society.

9. Are admissions open for Silver Oaks Mokila?+

Yes. Enquiries are open for the 2027–28 academic year. Parents can register their interest through the official Silver Oaks website.

10. How can I apply for admission to Silver Oaks Mokila?+

Parents can begin by filling out the online enquiry form on the Silver Oaks website. The admissions team will guide them through the subsequent steps, including the application process and campus visit.

11. Can parents visit the Mokila campus before applying?+

Yes. Parents can connect with the admissions team to enquire about campus visits and understand the school's learning philosophy, curriculum and environment.
